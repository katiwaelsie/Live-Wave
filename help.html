Frequently Asked Questions

How do I buy a ticket for a live event?
Click on any event on the homepage. If the event is premium, you will see an M-Pesa prompt. Enter your phone number, click pay, and enter your M-Pesa PIN on your phone. The stream will unlock instantly once payment is confirmed.
How do I get paid as a creator?
Your earnings accumulate in your Creator Dashboard. LiveConcerts takes a 15% commission on all ticket sales. You can request an M-Pesa payout at any time from the Earnings tab.
What equipment do I need to go live?
You can stream directly using your mobile phone or browser camera, professional DSLR or camcorders via HDMI capture cards, or professional streaming software like OBS Studio, vMix, and Streamlabs using your unique RTMP / WHIP keys.
Can I share my ticket link with a friend?
No. For paid events, the video player uses secure, single-use tokens generated specifically for your account. If you share the link, it will not work for anyone else.
How do I report a problem or bug?
Use the "Report a Bug / Feedback" link in the sidebar or click below to submit a ticket. Our engineering team reviews all reports within 24 hours.

Still need help?

Our support team is available Monday - Friday, 9AM to 5PM EAT.
